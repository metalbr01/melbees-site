Ready-to-use descriptions

Short description

Match and discard letters to uncover secrets in a Hangman and Minesweeper mix.

How it plays

Hangman meets Minesweeper. On each hex you choose to match or discard the letter, and a mistake costs a petal. The Pollen number tells you how many correct letters are neighbors of that hex.

Long description

Melbees blends Hangman and Minesweeper on a honeycomb. Every Pollen number is a clue: it tells you how many correct letters are neighbors of that hex. Use that logic to decide whether each letter is one to match or to discard. A mistake costs a petal. There are 28 categories with thousands of secrets, from geography and animals to music, film and science. Every secret uncovered comes with a fun fact and joins the player's collection in the Apiary of Secrets. Each phase hides 1, 2, 3 or 4 secret words. Three ways to play: Run (10 phases, against the clock), Walk (1 phase, just for fun) and the Daily Challenge (10 phases), the same for every player in the same language. The game is available in English, Portuguese, Spanish and French.

Who made it

About the developer

Melbee Games is Leo, an independent developer who built Melbees on his own. The idea combines two games almost everyone knows, Hangman and Minesweeper: the letters come from Hangman, and the deduction comes from the numbers, just like Minesweeper. Before opening to everyone, the game went through a closed test on Google Play.

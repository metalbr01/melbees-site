Do GTA games have speedruns? The 5 old ones, board by board

Vice City in 7:47, and there's a catch. Categories, records and crowd size on the GTA III, Vice City, San Andreas, IV and V speedrun boards.

By Melbees · Published on November 28, 2026 · Updated on November 28, 2026 · 3 min read

Vice City in 7 minutes 47? Hold on, there's a catch.

Pow

Seven minutes, 47 seconds. That's the Any% record for GTA Vice City on speedrun.com. It's real, and there's a catch (coming up). GTA VI came out on November 19 and it's all anyone talks about, but the speedrunners' GTA is a different one: Vice City, and it's not close.

Five games, five boards

GTA III, Vice City, San Andreas, IV and V have all had speedrun.com pages since November 2014, every one created between the 7th and the 16th. Counting full-game categories, III has 7, Vice City 8, IV 6, and San Andreas and V tie at 12. If "category" sounds weird, the Any%, 100% and glitchless guide sorts it out in two minutes.

The chart shows the speedrunners on each game's main board. When a board comes in a restricted version, like "No SSU", the one with the bigger crowd wins:

Vice City leads with 514 speedrunners, almost double GTA III. That's not a ranking of the best GTA, it's where the crowd is. The board's oldest run is from April 7, 2008, more than six years before the page even existed. And it's still alive: the board keeps only each person's best run, and 65 of those best runs are from 2026 (46 from 2025). People are running Vice City right now.

Game by game

  • GTA III (2001): Any% (No ASE) at 59min16s, set November 14, 2026, five days before GTA VI dropped. The 100% is 4h02min12s, with 47 speedrunners.
  • Vice City (2002): Any% (No SSU) at 47min54s and unrestricted Any% at 7min47s. The longer time is 6.15 times the shorter one. Only 58 people have a time on the unrestricted board, against 514 on the other. The 100% is 4h19min40s.
  • San Andreas (2004): 12 categories, from Any% and all story missions to tags, snapshots, horseshoes, oysters and "69/70 unique stunts". Any% (No AJS) at 2h49min05s, set November 13, six days before GTA VI. The 100% takes 10h27min06s, with 35 speedrunners.
  • GTA IV (2008): 3h19min51s for Any%, set February 26, 2023, with 78 speedrunners. The 100% (7h31min38s) has just 9.
  • GTA V (2013): Any% at 2h11min29s, set March 9, 2025, with 248 speedrunners and 357 runs on the board. The category list even has Director Mode and Golf.

The catch behind 7:47

Saw "7:47" and figured someone beats Vice City on a coffee break? Hang on. That time is from the board without "No SSU" in the name. The other one, at 47min54s, bans SSU, short for Script Stack Underflow.

Every tag like that is a ban on that board. In GTA III, "No ASE" means no Arbitrary Script Execution. In San Andreas, "No AJS" bans ASE plus Arbitrary Jump in Script. Same pattern everywhere: San Andreas has 32min03s on unrestricted Any% and 2h49min05s on "No AJS"; GTA III has 35min57s unrestricted and 59min16s on "No ASE". The full rules live on each board's page.

Golden rule: before you compare two times, read the board's name.

Is anyone still running these?

Yep, on very different boards. In 2026, GTA V Any% has 43 runs dated this year (29 from 2025), and San Andreas (No AJS) has 24 (12 from 2025). At the quiet end, GTA IV 100% has 9 speedrunners, with runs from 2016 to 2026 and only one from 2026. Meanwhile unrestricted San Andreas Any%, with 21 speedrunners, has every single run dated 2026.

And GTA VI? It already has a page on the site, but that's a different story, up next.

GTA VI: why was the wait so long and the hype so big?

Why did the wait for GTA VI stir so many people? A look at what makes anticipation huge, and how to make waiting worth it with a small daily game.

By Melbees · Published on November 22, 2026 · Updated on November 22, 2026 · 3 min read

Waiting is part of the game too.

Red

GTA VI arrived on November 19, 2026, a date confirmed by Take-Two, the company that owns Rockstar. For many people it ended a long wait: the previous main game in the series, GTA V, came out in 2013. The question we kept hearing during the countdown was always the same: why did this wait make so many people so eager? Here is a small indie studio's take, with no rumors and no numbers we could not confirm.

Thirteen years is a long time

When a much-loved game takes a long time to return, the memory of it grows. Someone who was 15 in 2013 is 28 today. Anticipation picks up layers: the game a person wants to play is no longer just the game, it is also who they were back then. That explains part of the size of the hype. The wait becomes a shared memory, and shared memory fuels conversation, memes and countdowns.

Counting days is a way of playing

We ran a countdown on our channels too. Countdowns work for three simple reasons:

  • They give you a finish line. Waiting with no date is tiring. Waiting with a date becomes a game: 30 days left, 7 days, 1 day.
  • They are shareable. Everyone counts together, which makes the wait feel like a party rather than lost time.
  • They make jokes. When anxiety rises, humor eases it. A lot of GTA VI memes are exactly that pressure valve.

The wait has its own look

Trailers, old covers, comparisons from one edition to the next. One of our pieces, "The Evolution", showed the series' covers in sequence, ending on a made-up "edition" of Melbees with Mel in sunglasses on the honeycomb. The joke says something true: we follow a series the way we follow a person growing up. Each cover marks an era, and seeing them together is a way to scratch the nostalgia itch.

The less fun side of hype

High expectations also send a bill. After launch, the conversation moved to things like frame rates on consoles, price (the Standard edition was listed at R$ 449.90 in Brazil, according to Tecnoblog and MeuPS), install size, and the playful nickname "Grand Theft Errands" that fans gave to the everyday chores inside the game. None of that takes anything away from anyone: it is simply what happens when many people want something badly. The bigger the wish, the closer the look at the details.

How to make the wait worthwhile

  1. Accept the time. If the install is slow, use that time for something light instead of refreshing the page.
  2. Swap the anxiety for a small habit. A 5-minute logic game, for example, keeps your head busy and ends by itself.
  3. Talk about it. Waiting is lighter when shared: send the countdown to a friend and plan to play together.

The rule

It fits in one sentence

Melbees blends Hangman and Minesweeper on a honeycomb. Every Pollen number is a clue: it tells you how many correct letters are neighbors of that hex. Use that logic to decide whether each letter is one to match or to discard. A mistake costs a petal.

  1. Hand out one sheet per student or per pair. It takes 10 to 20 minutes.
  2. Students mark ✓ or ✕ on each closed hex: ✓ to match (the letter is in the secret), ✕ to discard. At the end, they write down both secrets.
  3. To grade, use the answer key on the last pages: each wrong mark costs a petal.
  4. Then, talk about the words: the answer key gives the definition of each one.

The Melbees: The Secret of Bees Privacy Policy explains what information the game handles, how it handles it and what your choices are.

It applies to the game on Google Play (Android), the Microsoft Store (Windows) and the App Store (iPhone), to the guest-only browser edition and to the Daily Challenge that anyone can play in the browser. What differs in each version is gathered in its own sections.

The policy covers:

  • what stays only on your device;
  • children, teens and the age question;
  • ads and in-app purchases;
  • the account, when one is required, and the online ranking;
  • analytics and crash reports;
  • feedback and support;
  • the page for parents and guardians;
  • who the data is shared with, and how long it's kept;
  • your rights, and how to delete your data and your account.

This page is only a guide. The full text of the policy is what applies: Privacy Policy. The last-updated date is at the top, and there's also a Portuguese version.

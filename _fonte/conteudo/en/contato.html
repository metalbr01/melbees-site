To reach Melbee Games, write to contact@melbees.games.

Good reasons to write:

  • a question about the rules or a problem in the game;
  • a word, tip or fun fact that looks wrong (in the game, you can also use Send feedback, under Settings → Account, or the word report on the results screen);
  • a request about privacy, your data or your account;
  • press and interviews (the press kit has the fact sheet, copy and images);
  • schools and teachers using Paper Honeycomb;
  • an idea for the game.

The more detail, the better: tell us your device, your language and what happened.

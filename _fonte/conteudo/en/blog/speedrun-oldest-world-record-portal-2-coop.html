Speedrun records usually do not last. The time drops when someone finds a shortcut, polishes a route, or simply has a perfect afternoon. But some records stay. We went looking, using the public speedrun.com API, for the oldest world record still standing across the main categories of 10 well-known games. The answer came from a place many people would not expect: Portal 2's co-op mode.

The record

  • Game and category: Portal 2, Cooperative Game (the two-player campaign).
  • Time: 7 minutes and 48.2 seconds (468.2 s).
  • Date of the run: February 16, 2019.
  • Who: a pair of runners. As in any co-op mode, the record belongs to both.
  • How long it has stood: 2,836 days as of November 22, 2026, or 7 years, 9 months and 6 days.

How long is 2,836 days?

For a yardstick, we measured the age of every full-game world record the query returned: 48 valid records across 10 games. The median age is 280 days, and 29 of the 48 were set within the last year. The Portal 2 co-op record is 10 times older than the median. Only four records on the list predate 2022, and the oldest of them leads.

Why can a record last this long?

The data does not explain it, so treat what follows as reasonable hypotheses, not facts. First: co-op needs two people in sync, and agreeing on schedule, route and practice between two runners is harder than training alone. Second: a run under eight minutes is already short, and the shorter the run, the harder it is to find gains that are left. Third: the community's attention may be concentrated on other categories. See the contrast inside the same game: the single-player record is 36 minutes and 26.2 seconds, and it was set on March 23, 2026, more than seven years later.

The relation between the two categories is also curious. The single-player campaign takes 4.7 times as long as the co-op one. Two people, yet a much shorter run: a reminder that "more players" does not mean "more time".

How to read "still standing"

A record stands as long as nobody beats it on the leaderboard. A few details change how to read that:

  • The date the site keeps is the date of the run, not of submission or verification.
  • We looked only at full-game categories and left out miscellaneous categories and individual levels.
  • We analysed 10 games chosen by us. A game that is not on the list may have an older record: our result is the oldest in the sample.
  • A little-contested category can look "frozen" simply because few people run it.
What is "real time" in co-op?

For this game, speedrun.com uses real time, measured from the outside, from the start to the end of the campaign. There is no in-game timer that skips loading screens like in some other games on the list.

Can I check the run?

Yes. Every speedrun.com run has its own page, with video and comments. The address of the record run is in the data file of our query; we do not reproduce the video here.

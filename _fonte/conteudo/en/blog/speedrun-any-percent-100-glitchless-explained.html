Hollow Knight has two world records. One is 4:29.34, the other is 26:38. Same speedrun.com board, both legit. That's not a mess, that's what keeps the race fair: every category is a different rule for "beating the game", and every rule gets its own record.

The names you'll see on every board

  • Any% ("any percent"): beat it as fast as the rules allow, no matter how much of the game you pick up along the way. Usually one of the shortest.
  • 100%: beat it while doing everything the game counts as "complete". And that "everything" changes from game to game.
  • Glitchless: beat it without the glitches the community banned. The banned list is per game, too.

There are tons more. The name is just a nickname: the category description on the board is what rules.

Why there isn't just one record

Because you can only compare runs that play by the same rules. On a single board, the wildest shortcut would always win, and nobody running clean would ever show up. Split it by category and every race is fair.

Same games, wildly different times

The records as of November 22, 2026:

  • Hollow Knight: Any%, 4:29.34; All Skills (grab every ability), 26:38. Almost 6 times longer.
  • Portal: Out of Bounds (glitch your way out of the map), 5:00.735; Inbounds (stay inside the map), 7:13.425; Glitchless, 14:23.43. Glitchless takes 2.9 times as long as Out of Bounds.
  • Celeste: Any%, 24:44.083; 100%, 1:37:16.372. The 100% run takes 3.9 times as long.
  • Super Mario 64: here the categories go by star count. 0 Star, 6:14.19; 120 Star, 1:35:14. Fifteen times longer.

Every one of those is a world record. Each in its own category.

And there's one more layer: each board picks which stopwatch counts. The Hollow Knight Any% record is 4:29.34 with loading screens cut out, and 6:38 on a plain wall clock.

GTA VI launched on November 19 (that's the date from Take-Two), and speedrun.com, the site where people log speedrun records, already has a page for it. Runs? Not one. We checked today, November 22, 2026, day three after launch, and everything below is just what the site's public API gave back.

What the page has right now

  • Page created on November 9, 2026, ten days before launch.
  • Categories: zero. No Any%, no 100%.
  • Submitted runs: zero, not even ones waiting for review.
  • Game rules on the site: real time only, video and verification required, no milliseconds. The page also allows emulators.
  • Listed platforms: PlayStation 5, PS5 Pro, Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S.
  • Moderation: 3 moderators on the list.

That's a snapshot from 11/22/2026, and it can change any hour.

How long did other big launches take?

We picked five big releases and asked the API how many days passed before the first verified run, meaning one a moderator approved. There are two ways to count it. One uses the date the run was played, the other uses the date it was submitted. The first one only tells you the oldest run among those verified today, not when it showed up or got approved, so the chart uses submission dates.

By submission date, the first verified run for those five games landed between 6 and 28 days after launch, with a median of 15. By the date the runs were played, it's 1 to 25 days. In every case the game's page existed before launch, 8 to 31 days before; GTA VI's was created 10 days ahead. And GTA VI, on the day we looked, was on day 3 with no categories at all. The data doesn't say what comes next, and five games don't make a rule.

Console and PC, and the talk that comes later

The game's listing already has a platform variable with Console and PC options, but with no categories there's no board for it yet. Moderators are the ones who create categories and rules. The older GTAs, whose boards are full, are in our piece on the five old games.

Can we tell if GTA VI will get an Any% category?

No. Today the page has no categories at all. Moderators create them and set the rules, and that can change overnight.

Until GTA VI's board gets off zero, the Melbees Daily Challenge is a race against the word of the day: daily.melbees.games.

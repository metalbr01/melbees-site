One of the most shared jokes after GTA VI launched was about the friend, partner or roommate who "vanished" into the game: the classic "just one more mission". Our version became the video "Single-player": Mel at a table for two, the other chair taken by a phone, and her answer is to call the person back to multiplayer. This text is the serious side of the joke: how to play Melbees as a pair or as a team, in the browser, with nothing to install.

Why play with company

Social games bring people closer. After a night alone in a giant world, a short match with someone you know helps restart the conversation. A logic game has one extra perk: you both talk about what you thought, not only about what happened. "Why did you open that hex?" is a good way to start a chat.

Duel: one against the other

The Duel puts you and a friend in the same challenge, live. It comes in two flavors:

  • Shared honeycomb: you both work on the same honeycomb and compete for points.
  • Race: each of you plays your own honeycomb with the same Secrets, and whoever finishes first wins.

To start, open the Daily Challenge in your browser, go to the Duel & Co-op tab, tap to create a room and send the code to your friend. They tap to find a room, type the code, and the match begins. Choose the level together, among Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced and Expert, to keep the match fair.

Co-op: both on the same side

What if, instead of competing, you want to solve the honeycomb together? The Cooperative mode is for that: you play on the same team against the challenge. Creating and finding a room work just like in the Duel, and talking during the match is half the fun. It suits people who prefer helping to win over beating the other.

A quick reminder of how to play

Melbees is Hangman crossed with Minesweeper on a honeycomb. On each hex you pick Match, when you think the letter is right, or Discard, when you think it is not in the Secret. The action opens the hex: a flower shows a right letter, and a number, the Pollen, counts how many right letters are neighbors. A wrong action costs a petal.

Tips for your first match as a pair

  1. Start with the zeros. A 0 means no neighbor holds a right letter, and the game opens those neighbors for you.
  2. Agree on a talking rule. In Co-op, speak out loud before you act. In the Duel, save the chat for the end.
  3. Pick the level for the newest player. If one of you is just starting, Beginner makes the match fairer.
Question: what is the difference between the race and the shared honeycomb in the Duel?

In the shared honeycomb, both players work on the same one and compete for points. In the race, each plays their own honeycomb with the same Secrets, and whoever finishes first wins.

And GTA VI?

It is only the topic of the moment: we have no connection to Rockstar. If you and your friends play GTA VI together, great. For when you need a light, short break, the Duel and Co-op are one code away.

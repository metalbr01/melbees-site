Melbees

Independent games to play in English, Portuguese, Spanish and French.

Melbees is an independent studio that makes logic and word games. The main one, The Secret of Bees, mixes Hangman and Minesweeper: you match and discard letters to uncover secret words. The Daily Challenge runs in your browser, no installation needed.

On the blog we look at game mechanics, randomness and programming, and publish posts on GTA, speedrunning, science and unusual facts. Melbees Dev is the pen name of the studio's developer on the posts signed that way. For the studio and contact details, see About and Contact.

  • Daily Challenge: a new challenge every day.
  • Minesweeper with Hug's hints: the classic, with a mascot who walks you through the reasoning when you're stuck.
  • Blog: GTA, speedrunning, randomness in games, unusual facts and science.
